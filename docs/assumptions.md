# RetireWise AI — Baseline Assumptions & Disclaimers

## 1. Baseline Economic Parameters (Pakistan Macro)

- **Default Currency**: PKR (Pakistan Rupee).
- **Default Inflation Assumption**: 8.0% per annum (customizable by adviser).
- **Default Portfolio Nominal Return**: 11.0% per annum.
- **Default Life Expectancy / Planning Horizon**: Age 85.
- **Emergency Reserve Target**: 6 months of essential living expenses held in liquid instruments.

## 2. Tax & Legal Boundary in MVP

The MVP models pre-tax cash flows or post-tax net figures as entered by the financial adviser. A complete localized statutory income tax calculation engine (Section 12 of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001) is slated for post-MVP Roadmap V2.

## 3. Disclaimers

RetireWise AI is an open-source financial decision-support tool. It does not provide autonomous, regulated investment advice or guaranteed future outcomes. Advisers are required to perform independent suitability verification prior to executing client recommendations.
