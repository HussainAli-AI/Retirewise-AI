# RetireWise AI — Risk & Capacity Methodology

## 1. Distinction Between Risk Tolerance and Capacity for Loss

RetireWise AI explicitly decouples two concepts frequently conflated in retail advisory:
- **Risk Tolerance (Attitudinal)**: Psychological willingness to accept market fluctuations and paper losses.
- **Capacity for Loss (Objective)**: Financial ability to withstand drawdowns without endangering baseline food, shelter, and healthcare needs.

---

## 2. Attitudinal Risk Tolerance Scoring

Evaluated via an 8-question psychometric instrument covering:
1. Loss reaction & quarterly drawdown behavior
2. Comfort with monthly portfolio volatility
3. Historical investment experience (Bank accounts vs. Equities/Sukuk)
4. Financial literacy regarding inflation and real returns
5. Investment horizon perspective
6. Preference trade-off between return volatility and capital guarantees
7. Capital preservation vs. growth priorities
8. Inflation erosion vs. market loss trade-off

### Categorization Scale (0 - 100)
- **0 - 20**: Very Conservative
- **21 - 40**: Conservative
- **41 - 60**: Moderate
- **61 - 80**: Growth
- **81 - 100**: Aggressive

---

## 3. Objective Capacity for Loss Scoring

Calculated deterministically using a 100-point composite scoring formula:
1. **Income Dependency (30 pts)**:
   $$\text{Dependency Ratio} = \frac{\text{Retirement Expenses} - \text{Guaranteed Income}}{\text{Retirement Expenses}}$$
   $$\text{Score} = (1 - \text{Dependency Ratio}) \times 30$$
2. **Liquid Emergency Reserve (25 pts)**:
   Scaled up to 12 months of essential expenditure coverage.
3. **Solvency & Debt-to-Assets (20 pts)**:
   Encumbrances above 50% reduce points to zero.
4. **Human Capital & Recovery Horizon (15 pts)**:
   Pre-retirement clients receive points for remaining earning years. Retired clients receive 0 points.
5. **Dependent Drag (10 pts)**:
   Financial commitments to dependents reduce capital resilience.

---

## 4. Conflict Detection Rule

When a client exhibits:
$$\text{Risk Category} \in \{\text{Growth}, \text{Aggressive}\} \quad \text{AND} \quad \text{Capacity Category} \in \{\text{Very Low}, \text{Low}\}$$

RetireWise AI flags a **Critical Suitability Conflict**. Regulatory and fiduciary best practices require that investment asset allocation be strictly bound to **Capacity for Loss**.
